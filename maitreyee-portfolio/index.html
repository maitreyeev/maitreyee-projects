Selected Case Studies

Three problems, worked end to end.

Turning a 3 step KYC journey into 2

Problem
  • KYC was a critical part of onboarding but involved an outdated 3-step PAN and Aadhaar verification flow
  • Needed to improve the user experience without compromising compliance requirements
What I investigated
  • Analysed where users experienced friction in the onboarding journey
  • Evaluated the purpose of each individual verification step
  • Considered operational, technical, and compliance constraints
  • Compared alternative verification approaches, including DigiLocker
Product decision
  • Integrated DigiLocker into the onboarding journey
  • Reduced the PAN and Aadhaar verification flow from 3 steps to 2
Outcome
  • Reached 10,000+ monthly KYC completions
  • Created a simpler, faster onboarding experience

Making bank verification pennyless

Problem
  • Penny-drop bank account verification was the default method for every user, but it carried a real transaction cost on each verification
  • Needed a lower-cost verification method without compromising reliability or compliance
What I investigated
  • Analysed every user onboarded on the platform to understand bank-verification eligibility patterns
  • Found that 96% of users were eligible for pennyless bank account verification
  • Evaluated the reliability and coverage of pennyless verification against penny-drop
  • Considered how to handle the remaining ineligible users without breaking onboarding
Product decision
  • Designed and implemented pennyless bank account verification end to end
  • Made pennyless the default verification method, with penny-drop retained as a fallback for the remaining ineligible users
  • Separately renegotiated vendor contracts, reducing overall KYC spend by a further 30%
Outcome
  • Cut the cost previously spent on penny-drop verification by 76%
  • Kept 100% verification coverage via the penny-drop fallback for the remaining 4% of users
  • Vendor renegotiation contributed an additional 30% reduction in overall KYC spend

Product Approach

How I think about products.

Start with the problem

  • Understand what the user is actually trying to accomplish
  • Identify where the current experience breaks
  • Ask what happens if we do nothing

Use data to find the signal

  • Analyse behaviour and funnel data
  • Identify friction and opportunities
  • Size problems before investing in solutions
  • Measure whether a product decision actually changed an outcome

Make trade offs explicit

  • Balance user experience, business impact, engineering effort, compliance, risk, and time
  • Make assumptions and constraints visible
  • Avoid optimising for a single metric at the expense of the overall experience

Build with the team

  • Work closely with engineering, design, operations, compliance, and external partners
  • Translate ambiguous problems into clear product requirements
  • Create alignment across teams with different goals and constraints

Measure what changed

  • Treat shipping as the beginning, not the end
  • Define success metrics before launch
  • Measure behavioural and business outcomes after launch
  • Use learnings to inform the next iteration
